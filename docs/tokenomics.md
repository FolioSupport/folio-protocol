# Folio tokenomics

## Status

No token contract has been announced for Folio. The website shows the contract slot at the top of every page as an inert placeholder until an address is stated, and it will not render any address that has not been stated in the project register. This document therefore describes the principles that any token design must satisfy. It contains no supply figure, price, allocation percentage or schedule, because none has been published. When figures are announced they will be added here and in the register together, and not before.

## Anything circulating is unverified

Until the official address appears in the contract bar on the website, treat every address shared on social media, in chats or in replies as unverified. The bar is the single place the project will publish it.

## Purpose of a token

A token only earns a place in this system if it does work the product cannot do without it. Three candidate roles are under consideration, none committed:

1. **Access and tiers.** Heavier use of the dossier features, such as bulk lookups and history exports, could be gated by holding or spending the token.
2. **Dispute bonds.** A reader who disputes a tag could post a small bond that is returned when the dispute is upheld, which discourages spam without closing the door on good faith corrections.
3. **Contributor rewards.** People who supply verified evidence that improves a record could be rewarded in proportion to how often that evidence is relied on.

The token is not a claim on revenue, and it does not buy influence over what a tag says. Tag outcomes follow evidence only.

## Allocation framework

When numbers exist they will be organised under the following headings, each with a stated size, a stated vesting rule and a stated rationale:

* community and contributor rewards,
* liquidity and market making,
* the team and future hires,
* treasury for audits, infrastructure and data costs,
* ecosystem grants on Robinhood Chain.

## Rules the design must follow

* **Transparent vesting.** Team and treasury allocations vest on a public schedule with on-chain locks that anyone can inspect.
* **Disclosed liquidity.** The pair, the amount and the lock status are published at launch.
* **No hidden mint.** Supply is fixed or follows a rule that is written down before launch. Mint permissions, if any, are stated in the contract documentation.
* **No special tags.** Holders, team members and partners are scored by the same rules as everyone else.
* **Single announcement point.** The address, the supply and the schedule are published together, from the official channels listed on the website, in one message.

## What would change this document

A stated contract address, a stated supply, or a published vesting contract. Each is recorded in the register as a separate fact with its own state, so the website and these notes can never disagree about which facts are confirmed.

## Risk notes

Tokens carry the risk of total loss. Early liquidity is thin and prices can move sharply on small trades. Lookalike contracts appear within minutes of any announcement. Read the contract bar, compare the address character by character, and use Folio's own lookalike view before buying anything.
